## Honest verdict

ReferralArc’s differentiated contribution is capability-lifetime consent: visible exact authorization changes the browser’s native WebMCP surface. Referral coordination, AI scheduling, dynamic tools, and consent management are existing concepts, so the submission should claim the specific application and execution—not invention of those categories. No implementation can guarantee a prize; the goal is to make the strongest defensible contribution obvious, working, and easy to judge.
